# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Technical Lead

### Role Summary
Technical Leads provide technical direction for projects, own architectural decisions, and mentor junior team members. They bridge engineering leadership and hands-on development work.

### Responsibilities
- Evaluate technical approaches and recommend architecture patterns
- Conduct design reviews and provide technical guidance
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigations
- Mentor junior developers and support skill development
- Collaborate with Product Manager on technical feasibility of requirements
- Participate in planning to estimate technical complexity

### Goals
- Deliver maintainable, scalable solutions
- Build technical capability across the team
- Reduce technical debt and risk
- Enable faster delivery through architectural clarity

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **With Developers**: Provides technical direction and code review leadership
- **With Project Managers**: Advises on technical feasibility and implementation trade-offs
- **With Product Managers**: Collaborates on solution design and technical requirements
- **With QA/Testing**: Partners on testability and quality strategy

### Typical Communication
- Design review meetings and architectural discussions
- Code review comments and mentoring sessions
- Planning sessions and technical feasibility assessments
- Risk identification and mitigation discussions

---

## UX/Design Lead

### Role Summary
UX/Design Leads own user experience strategy, design interfaces, and ensure usability standards are met. They advocate for the end user throughout the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Define UX strategy and design system
- Create wireframes, prototypes, and visual designs
- Conduct user research and usability testing
- Establish design standards and accessibility requirements
- Review implementation for design fidelity
- Collaborate with product on feature prioritization from UX perspective

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, accessible user experiences
- Validate assumptions through user research
- Build consistency across product surfaces
- Reduce support burden through better design

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **With Developers**: Partners on implementation and component design
- **With Product Managers**: Collaborates on feature prioritization and user needs
- **With Project Managers**: Provides design estimates and participates in planning
- **With QA/Testing**: Partners on usability testing and acceptance criteria

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and component handoffs
- User research findings and validation sessions
- Design system documentation and standards
- Usability testing coordination and feedback

---

## Quality Assurance Lead

### Role Summary
QA Leads manage QA strategy, define test plans, and own quality metrics for projects. They ensure quality standards are met and tracked throughout the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy and test plan for each project
- Establish quality gates and acceptance criteria
- Own and track quality metrics (defect rate, coverage, etc.)
- Manage QA team assignments and coordination
- Identify quality risks and propose mitigations
- Participate in planning to define QA approach and effort

### Goals
- Achieve target quality levels efficiently
- Prevent defects through strategic testing
- Enable fast, confident releases
- Build quality culture across the team

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **With Developers**: Partners on test design and quality standards
- **With Project Managers**: Advises on quality risks and test effort
- **With Product Managers**: Collaborates on acceptance criteria and success metrics
- **With Technical Lead**: Partners on testability and architecture

### Typical Communication
- Test plan reviews and quality gate discussions
- Quality metrics reporting and trend analysis
- Defect triage and root cause discussions
- Planning sessions and risk assessments

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters facilitate agile ceremonies, remove team impediments, and coach the team on agile practices. They enable team productivity and continuous improvement.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate daily standups, planning, and retrospectives
- Identify and help remove team blockers
- Coach team on agile practices and continuous improvement
- Maintain sprint/iteration health and velocity tracking
- Shield team from interruptions and scope creep
- Escalate impediments that require management attention

### Goals
- Maximize team velocity and predictability
- Foster psychological safety and continuous improvement
- Remove organizational blockers to delivery
- Enable sustainable team performance

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **With All Roles**: Facilitates ceremonies and removes impediments
- **With Project Managers**: Partners on planning and risk management
- **With Developers**: Supports team health and productivity

### Typical Communication
- Ceremony facilitation (standups, planning, retros)
- Impediment tracking and escalation
- Team health and velocity discussions
- Continuous improvement coaching sessions

---

## Release Manager

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate release planning, manage deployment schedules, and oversee release communication. They ensure smooth, coordinated transitions to production.

### Responsibilities
- Plan and schedule releases across teams and dependencies
- Prepare release notes and deployment documentation
- Coordinate staging and production deployments
- Manage release communication to stakeholders and support
- Track release metrics and post-release issues
- Coordinate rollback and incident response

### Goals
- Execute reliable, on-time releases
- Minimize release-related incidents
- Ensure clear communication during deployments
- Enable faster, more frequent releases

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **With Project Managers**: Partners on release planning and scheduling
- **With Developers**: Coordinates deployment and verification
- **With QA/Testing**: Ensures quality gates are met before release
- **With Technical Lead**: Coordinates architectural changes and data migrations

### Typical Communication
- Release planning and scheduling coordination
- Deployment checklists and runbooks
- Release notes and stakeholder communications
- Post-release metrics and incident response

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
Business Analysts translate business requirements into technical specifications and validate solutions with stakeholders. They bridge business needs and technical implementation.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and analyze business requirements
- Document requirements in technical specifications
- Define acceptance criteria with stakeholders
- Validate solutions meet business needs
- Identify business impact of technical decisions
- Support stakeholder communication and change management

### Goals
- Ensure solutions address actual business problems
- Reduce rework and misalignment
- Enable stakeholders to validate solutions early
- Improve communication between business and technical teams

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **With Product Managers**: Partners on requirement gathering and validation
- **With Developers**: Clarifies requirements and validates implementation
- **With Project Managers**: Supports stakeholder management and communication
- **With QA/Testing**: Collaborates on acceptance criteria and validation

### Typical Communication
- Requirements gathering and analysis sessions
- Specification documentation and review
- Stakeholder communication and feedback sessions
- Acceptance criteria definition and validation
